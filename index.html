About

Aspiring to build sustainable futures for all

I work at the intersection of climate action, governance and community systems, helping translate complex policy frameworks into implementation that is practical, equitable and lasting.

My work spans research, programme management and field implementation across India, engaging actors from governments to grassroots communities. I'm particularly interested in how climate solutions can remain coherent across institutional, geographic and social scales.

I'm drawn to questions of institutional design: how do we build the administrative capacity, financial mechanisms and political legitimacy necessary for communities to lead their own climate transitions.
